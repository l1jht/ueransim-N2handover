IMS SMS drill - measured summary (free5GC + IMS testbed, 2026-09-30)
===================================================================

Prerequisite tweak: pyhss/default_ifc.xml - the SIP MESSAGE iFC ServerName
  sip:smsc.ims.mnc001.mcc001.3gppnetwork.org:5060 -> :7090
  (the SMSC listens on 7090; fixed by deploy/open5gs/tweaks/apply-open5gs-tweaks.sh
  tweak #4; after the change: restart pyhss + scscf, then re-register both UEs
  so the S-CSCF fetches the updated iFC).

UE1 (imsi-001010000000001) -> UE2 (imsi-001010000000002):
  nr-cli imsi-001010000000001 -e "ims-sms sip:001010000000002@ims.mnc001.mcc001.3gppnetwork.org hello-ueransim-sms-0930"
  ue1: "SMS result: delivered (202)"; SMSC RP-ACK received
  ue2: "SMS received from 001010000000001 [tpdu]: hello-ueransim-sms-0930"
       smsHistory: "001010000000001: hello-ueransim-sms-0930"

UE2 -> UE1 (reply):
  ue2: "SMS result: delivered (202)"
  ue1 smsHistory: "001010000000002: reply-from-ue2-ok"

SMSC log (3GPP path):
  MESSAGE ... SMS_FROM_3GPP: FROM 001010000000001 / TO 001010000000002
  TEXT hello-ueransim-sms-0930 / DCS 8 (UCS2)
  SMS_WORKER: delivered via the TS 24.341 path (application/vnd.3gpp.sms)

Conclusion: the ueransim-ims SMS capability works on the combined n2handover+ims
build (binary 3GPP path; the pjsua text/plain compatibility tweak is NOT needed).
