Open5GS vs free5GC - IMS call continuity across N2 handovers (10 round trips each)
=================================================================================

Protocol: identical IMS stack/UE client/measurement (rtpengine-side capture, port-based
          classification by media port 50002/50003, 60 ms interruption threshold).

metric                             Open5GS (baseline)     free5GC (Plan L)
handovers / call kept              20 / 20 CONFIRMED      20 / 20 CONFIRMED
RTP gap max, ue1 DL (ms)           40.9                   41.0
RTP gap max, ue1 UL (ms)           41.2                   40.6
RTP gap max, ue2 DL (ctrl, ms)     41.3                   40.6
RTP gap max, ue2 UL (ctrl, ms)     40.9                   40.9
gaps > 60 ms                       0                      0
prep req->reqrecv (ms)             11-13                  21-27
ran recfg->recfgcmpl (ms)          0-1                    0-1
exec cmd->notify (ms)              0-1                    0-1
release notify->ctxrel (ms)        501-508                207-219

Notes: the IMS side (domain, stack, UE client, media parameters) is identical in both
environments; only the 5GC differs (free5GC UPF additionally masquerades the media,
classification by UE-side media port is invariant to that). RTP-level continuity is
equivalent; the control-plane timing differences reflect the two AMF/SMF implementations
(free5GC: ~2x prepare round trip, ~2.4x faster source release).
